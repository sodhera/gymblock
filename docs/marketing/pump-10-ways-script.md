# GymBlock short video: "10 ways to get that pump"

Format: vertical, 45–55 s, fast cuts, on-screen captions for every line, countdown from 10 to 1. Number 1 is GymBlock. Voice is a friend talking, not a coach shouting. No body-outcome promises; the only claims are the approved ones from onboarding.

## Hook (0–3 s)

**Visual:** You, mid-set, phone face down on the bench next to you. Caption: "10 ways to get that pump."
**VO:** "Ten ways to actually get a pump in the gym. Number one is the one nobody does."

## 10. Warm up the muscle, not the whole gym (3–7 s)

**Visual:** One light set of the first exercise, slow.
**VO:** "Ten. One light set of the lift you're about to do. Blood goes where you send it."

## 9. Slow the negative (7–11 s)

**Visual:** Curl, three-second lowering.
**VO:** "Nine. Three seconds down on every rep. The lowering half is where it burns."

## 8. Squeeze at the top (11–15 s)

**Visual:** Cable fly or chest press, one-second hold.
**VO:** "Eight. Pause and squeeze at the top. One second. Feel it or it didn't count."

## 7. Stop locking out (15–19 s)

**Visual:** Leg press or push-up, stopping just short of straight.
**VO:** "Seven. Don't lock out. Keep tension on the muscle the whole set."

## 6. Drop the weight, raise the reps (19–23 s)

**Visual:** Lateral raises, light dumbbells, 15+ reps.
**VO:** "Six. Lighter weight, twelve to twenty reps. Chasing a pump is not chasing a PR."

## 5. Finish with a drop set (23–27 s)

**Visual:** Rack of dumbbells, hand moving down one size.
**VO:** "Five. Last set, drop the weight and go again. No rest."

## 4. Drink water before, not just after (27–30 s)

**Visual:** Water bottle, quick sip between sets.
**VO:** "Four. Water. Half a litre before you start. Dehydrated muscle doesn't fill up."

## 3. Rest the right amount (30–35 s)

**Visual:** Rest timer on a wrist or phone face, 60 to 90 seconds. GymBlock's rest ring filling to 1:30, the buzz notification.
**VO:** "Three. Rest sixty to ninety seconds. Not four minutes because a video got good."

## 2. Think about the muscle (35–40 s)

**Visual:** Tight shot on a bicep during a curl. Caption: "Scrolling weakens your mind-muscle connection."
**VO:** "Two. Think about the muscle while it moves. Scrolling weakens your mind-muscle connection. Put the phone away and feel every rep."

## 1. Block your phone (40–50 s)

**Visual:** GymBlock Home, tap Start workout, Instagram opens to the GymBlock shield screen. Then an exercise page, a set logged, the rest ring.
**VO:** "One. Block your phone. GymBlock shields the apps you choose from Start workout to Finish, times your rests, and logs every set. Two minutes on your phone each rest can be thirty-four minutes of a forty-six-minute workout. That's your pump, gone."
**Caption under the number:** "Based on your own answers, not a measurement."

## Close (50–55 s)

**Visual:** Finish, the summary page, phone goes back in the bag.
**VO:** "Put it away. Hit the pump. GymBlock, on the App Store."
**Caption:** "GymBlock · Free workout or your split · Pro from $4.99/month"

## Notes for the edit

- Every number is a hard cut with a sound tick, numbers large in the top third, ink on paper to match the app.
- Show real app screens only. Blocking only works on a real iPhone, so capture the shield on a device, not the simulator.
- Keep the one-line qualifier on the phone-time stat. It is self-reported arithmetic from onboarding, never measured phone use.
- Do not add "gain X cm" or "bigger arms in Y weeks". The app never predicts body outcomes.
- Alternate hook if the first tests weak: "Your phone is killing your pump. Ten fixes."
- Shorter 30 s cut: keep 9, 8, 6, 3, 2, 1 and the close.
