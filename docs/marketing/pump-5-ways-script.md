# GymBlock short video: "5 ways to get that pump"

5 ways to get that pump in the gym.

1. Slow the negative. Three seconds down on every rep. The lowering half is where it burns.

2. Squeeze at the top. Hold for one second. If you can't feel it, you're not working it.

3. Block your phone. No scrolling between sets, no four-minute rests, no losing the pump you just built.

4. Go lighter, go longer. Twelve to twenty reps. A pump is not a PR.

5. Finish with a drop set. Last set, drop the weight, go again. No rest.

Put it away. Hit the pump. GymBlock.
