# Credits

GymBlock uses Apple's SF Pro system font with Dynamic Type. DM Sans, bundled in earlier prototypes under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, was removed when the app adopted the onboarding's SF Pro type. The onboarding arm artwork is the user-supplied asset in `GymBlock/Assets.xcassets/OnboardingArm.imageset`.

## Sounds (onboarding)

- **iOS system sounds:** the Messages ding, the lock click, the tri-tone, the keyboard tick and tock, the key click, and the Health and Activity sounds Apple Watch uses ("reading failed", "goal attained") are played from iOS's own `/System/Library/Audio/UISounds` at run time. They aren't bundled.
- **`gb-womp-1…3.caf`:** "Trombone disappoint" from Mixkit (https://mixkit.co, item 744), under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License, level-matched and cut into its three notes. Not played since 18 Oct 2026 (Mind A uses Health's "reading failed"), but still bundled.
- **`gb-clink-1…3.caf`, `gb-plate-light/medium/heavy/drop.caf`, `gb-clank-dull.caf`:** from Kenney's "Impact Sounds" (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0, so attribution is appreciated but not required. The metal and plate impacts are converted and level-matched.
