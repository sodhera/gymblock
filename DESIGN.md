# GymBlock design

Onboarding follows **docs/ONBOARDING-V5-PLAN.md**. The app after onboarding follows **docs/WORKOUT-V6-PLAN.md** (V6 replaces the training and navigation parts of docs/UX-RESET-V3.md).

## Visual system

One look everywhere, set by onboarding: the *paper* stage (warm off-white, a faint peach light in the middle, quiet ink dots) behind every page, in light appearance. SF Pro with Dynamic Type; titles bold, labels medium, numbers monospaced. Liquid Glass on iOS 26 for every control: prominent white glass for the one primary action, glass steppers, rings, tiles, chooser rows and round icon buttons, with translucent fallbacks before iOS 26. Native Lists and Forms (settings, editors, history details) sit on the same stage under the native iOS 26 Liquid Glass navigation bar; Home and the workout put their controls in that bar as toolbar items rather than drawing their own.

Ink (#141417) is the action; emerald (#0F9A6B) is the signal and nothing else — the live set, a finished rest, today in the week, a like-for-like gain, the "on" state of a toggle, the phone arc in the estimate. Picker values are grey. Never a coloured background. (The red ember and a graphite-and-mint scheme were both retired on 8 Oct 2026; paper was chosen from three rendered directions.)

Keep 24-point margins and a fixed grid. The primary action sits in exactly the same place on Home, the workout and the summary (as on every onboarding page); a 20-point caption slot above it and a 44-point secondary slot below it are always reserved, so it never moves.

## Onboarding

Full spec: docs/ONBOARDING-V5-PLAN.md. Twenty pages (eighteen for Rarely), one question or idea each: welcome; name; gender; height; weight; phone between sets; minutes per rest; phone time against training time; hours a year as 45-minute workouts; paired story stages (mind-muscle, pump, progress); app blocking; rest alerts; hold to commit; offer; set up your splits (add, name, pick exercises; optional).

**Colour.** One *paper* stage on every page: warm off-white with a wide peach wash and a tighter core behind the stage area, ink dots that strengthen towards it (drawn once per screen size, `DotGrid`; every token lives in `JourneyTheme.paper`). No page uses a coloured background. Three surface tones: the stage, raised white glass (a faint top-lit rim on every card and control, `JourneyRim`), and the one ink action. Emerald (#0F9A6B) is the signal — ≤2% of a screen, always the one thing to look at: the phone arc, a finished rest, today, a gain, the check on a chosen answer. A chosen answer rises (brighter glass, ink edge) and is never a coloured block. Tracks, hairlines and quiet fills are ink at low opacity (`JourneyColor.ink(_:)`). Captions meet WCAG AA (≥ 4.5:1).

**Brand.** The mark is a kettlebell that is a padlock: the handle is the shackle, the bell is the body, and the keyhole is cut in the emerald signal, the only colour (`BrandMark`, the same geometry as `scripts/generate-app-icon.swift`). In the app it is ink on paper: the wordmark on the welcome page and the badge on the account and offer cards. The app icon is the mark in paper white on an ink field with a faint top light, strongest on any wallpaper. Chosen on 8 Oct 2026 from rendered options (a plate-dial combination lock, a stacked-plates lock) shown on ink, paper and emerald fields and on a Home Screen; two earlier marks were rejected. **Red.** One warm colour, `JourneyColor.danger` (#DB4036), exists for time lost to the phone: the phone arc on the reveal ring and the dots on the hours-a-year page; training is the emerald signal. Nothing else is red. The launch screen is the stage colour and the first page rises into it: no white cover, no flash.

**Type and scale.** Headlines are `.title` bold with slightly tight tracking, centred in a two-line box so a one-line headline never sits above a hole. Section labels (`Eyebrow`) are the subheadline style in sentence case, secondary colour; hero numbers are `.largeTitle` bold; no tracked capitals, no fixed-size display fonts; numbers are monospaced. Everything is compact: 50-pt primary button, 52-pt answer rows, 18–20-pt card radii, 16-pt card padding, a 160–220-pt ring, 26-pt stepper digits, 44-pt hero numbers.

**Material.** Liquid Glass (iOS 26) for every control: prominent white glass for the primary action, glass answer cards, back button, notification cards, tiles and info cards; translucent fallbacks before iOS 26.

**Motion and touch.** Calm, one clock per page change: everything that changes fades out (0.18 s), the page swaps unseen, everything fades in (0.35 s); a stage shared by two pages stays put. Low-bounce springs; pages slide 18 pt in the direction of travel as they fade (Back slides back); scenes start after the page settles; Continue fades in when a scene ends; Home's cards settle in one after another on first appearance. Selecting an answer only selects; Continue moves on. Haptics are designed per moment: iOS-style double taps for notifications, crescendos for counting, a landing thud for reveals, rising impacts for each set, a ramp for the hold.

**Illustration.** The approved arm artwork (pre-rendered poses) and the brain-and-nerve scene carry the story pages. Blocked apps are SF Symbols on glass, not borrowed logos.

**Voice.** Qualifiers stay, written as a person would say them: "Stays on this iPhone. Never shared.", "Illustrative, not a measurement.", "An example log, not your data.", "Blocking is a preview in this build: nothing is enforced yet.", "Subscriptions aren’t switched on in this build yet."

## Training and navigation

No tabs. Home, the workout or its summary fills the screen; a running workout always comes back first.

**Home** — the game, from logged workouts only. The bar title is the greeting ("Good evening, Sirish") with History and Settings. Every card opens a detail. A streak card: flame and the streak of consecutive weeks with a workout as a 44-pt number, a goal ring ("2/5 this week") and seven day dots; it opens History. An exercise carousel: one exercise per page, its best comparable set per workout as a line (reps or load held constant, the constant named under the title, the gain at the right), pages advance every 4 s with a slide, a swipe takes over and pauses the clock for 12 s, dots beneath the card; a page opens that exercise's progress. Two cards: time since the last PR (with the exercise and gain; opens its progress) and time training (all-time minutes and workout count; opens a minutes-per-week page). "Sample data" or "Example until your first workout" sits in the caption slot above Start, which has a 20-pt rest beneath it. A brand-new log shows a labelled example (`GymStore.exampleStats`, `exampleTrends`) until the first workout is saved, so Home never opens empty. No number appears twice. Then the blocking caption and **Start workout**, which opens the chooser: the splits (the one that's up next is marked, with an `…` edit/delete menu), Free workout, New split; tapping a row starts that workout. No workout name sits on Home. "Sample data" stays visible when demo history is loaded.

**Workout** — top bar (the workout clock doubles as Pause/Resume, Sets, End); exercise name + chevron (opens the picker) with set dots ("Set 2 of 3", target = last time's set count, else 3); the ring (ready: last time's best; set: the set clock; rest: fills to the rest length, then red with "Rest's up" and a double haptic; the length is changed in place); weight and reps glass steppers (plate-grid steps, hold to repeat, tap to type; typing hides the ring so the button stays above the keyboard); "Last set ›" for one-tap correction; one honest caption; the primary button. Start set → Finish set → rest → Start set; after last time's number of sets it becomes `Next: <exercise>` with `Another set` beneath (free workout: Next exercise; split done: Finish workout). A 0.6 s guard stops double taps. Zero reps finishes as a missed attempt. Settings → Time each set off makes it one tap per set (timing marked unknown, never guessed).

**Interruptions** — everything is a saved timestamp, so locking, calls and relaunches lose nothing. Rest's up is a local notification when locked; the Live Activity (Lock Screen and Dynamic Island) shows the set or rest clock, the next set and a Start/Finish button that carries its intent so a lagging display can't double-log. A workout idle for an hour gets a "Still training?" reminder and, on return, an offer to finish it at its last set. Implausible set clocks (< 3 s, or > 15 min for a rep set) are kept but marked unknown. **Pause** freezes the workout clock and the running rest or set (the ring dims, "Paused" in red, Resume becomes the primary action, rest alerts are held and the simulated block lifts); resume moves the anchors forward, so paused time never counts as training, rest or set time.

**Summary** — an eyebrow (split · date) and a headline that changes with the workout ("Stronger than last time, Sirish." when there is a gain, "First one in the log" for the first workout, otherwise "Nice work"), one hero card counting up weight moved (or reps) with a soft glow, three small tiles (time, sets, average rest), "Better than last time" (same split, reps or load held constant, red arrows), a per-exercise recap, Done; Save as split for free workouts. Ending a workout confirms with an alert whose Keep going is always visible.

History opens on a glass card with the period total and a weekly bar chart (this week in emerald; Reps / Weight moved pills), then exercise progress (the scope defaults to the most recent workout's; Before/After tiles, the difference as a 44-pt headline, an inline line chart, the records) and the workout list with full exercise names; a workout can be deleted from its detail page.

**Split editor and exercise picker** — the editor is a native list on the stage: the name as a title-sized field, numbered exercise rows with their area, drag to reorder, swipe to remove, an Add row, and a footer that says so. The picker groups the catalogue by area with a check that fills in the signal colour, a running count on Done, search, and an "Add “…”" row when the search finds nothing. Editing exposes weight/reps first and timing/date/warm-up under Details; unknown timing stays unknown. Settings order: workout (rest length, rest alert, time each set), blocking (simulated, labelled), you (name, body, units, language, haptics, sound), answers and data, account.

## Data and boundaries

Preserve local history, exercise/split IDs, actual reps, load conventions, timing provenance and sample markers. Demo loading is explicit/idempotent and never replaces existing work. Blocking remains simulated and is labelled wherever it appears; no live billing/account/backend service is configured. The Live Activity is local (no push).

Build success does not prove visual quality. Verify actual simulator layouts, full routes, keyboard and larger text, appearance, corrections, counter continuity and before/after records. Hardware haptic feel, physical-device handling and user comprehension need separate evidence.
