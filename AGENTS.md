# GymBlock workspace instructions

- Native Swift/SwiftUI only. `project.yml` is the XcodeGen source of truth; regenerate when adding files or changing configuration.
- Keep the app extremely simple. Since 8 Oct 2026 it has an account: Sign in with Apple or Google through Supabase Auth (project `tlcmpgxuyngsbjuhring`, tables prefixed `gb_`, schema in `supabase/migrations/`, dashboard steps in `supabase/README.md`), every workout, set, split, answer and setting synced to the account (`CloudSync.swift`), first-party analytics of every screen, tap, choice, set, purchase and error into `gb_events` (`Analytics.swift`; never names, free text or emails), and GymBlock Pro through RevenueCat (`Subscriptions.swift`, USD 4.99/month or 49.99/year, entitlement `pro`). The iPhone copy stays the working copy and the app must keep working offline. Development runs (`--demo`, `--skip-onboarding`, `--ui-reset`, `-journeyStep`, `-workoutStage`) are offline: no account, no analytics, device-only Log out / Delete. With an account, Log out clears the device copy (it returns at the next sign-in) and Delete account calls `gb_delete_account()` then wipes the device. No other SDKs.
- Follow docs/ONBOARDING-V8-PLAN.md for onboarding (since 20 Oct 2026; V7, then V5, for anything it doesn't change): it sells time back, from the person's own answers (the minutes scrolling runs past the rest they need, per rest, per workout, per year), never the pump; no graph on a question page; one question or idea per page, the *paper* stage on every page (warm off-white #F5F3EF, ink type, white Liquid Glass, an ink primary button, emerald #0F9A6B as the only signal; light appearance; chosen on 8 Oct 2026 from rendered options after the dark ember and graphite schemes were rejected) — never a coloured background, native Liquid Glass bars and controls, compact sizing (28-pt headlines, 50-pt buttons, 18–20-pt card radii), SF Pro Dynamic Type, calm motion, answers never auto-advance, restrained haptics on the beats that mean something (Core Haptics scores in `JourneyScores.swift`) and real sounds placed on their frames: iOS's own system sounds, plus licensed recordings in `Resources/Sounds` (CREDITS.md). Never synthesized, never other apps' audio, no background bed, a plain "we" voice (Sulav's founder's note was removed on 20 Oct 2026 at the user's request; the welcome is one message, "Focused workouts."), blocking and rest alerts before the commit and offer, splits chosen after the offer. Follow docs/WORKOUT-V6-PLAN.md for the app after onboarding: same paper stage and Liquid Glass (native navigation bars), a floating Liquid Glass tab bar with Home and Profile (since 11 Oct 2026, at the user's request; hidden during a workout and its summary), one primary action in one fixed place, everything saved as it happens, Live Activity for rests. `RootView` picks the screen from state, as in SleepBlock (launch hold → onboarding → standalone sign-in → account notice → offer → app; `AccountGate.swift`). Sign-up's account step ("Sign up with Apple/Google", or in offline Debug runs a labelled skip) sits between the commit and the offer; the welcome page's "I already have an account" opens the separate Welcome back screen ("Sign in with…"). Sign-up into an existing account says so; sign-in to an account with no GymBlock profile says it couldn't be found and offers Sign up. The offer is a wall (no Back, no close). The offer shows RevenueCat's monthly and yearly plans when `REVENUECAT_API_KEY` (Secrets.xcconfig) is set; with no key it says subscriptions are off and shows the labelled Debug-only skip, never in Release, never a pretend price. Keep one-line claim qualifiers. The pump and mind-muscle pages were removed on 20 Oct 2026 (the founder's point: people get a pump with their phone out, so the claim fails their own check); the rest they need is never counted as lost, every number rounds down, and nothing is shown that doesn't fit their own gym time. Do not invent body-outcome predictions.
- Home shows the split that's up next (splits rotate) or Free workout; Start workout turns on blocking (when it's on) and opens the split’s first exercise or Free workout exercise selection. Finish clears it before summary. Never invent a first-time load; mark implausible timing unknown rather than guessing.
- Splits may carry a colour the user picks in the split editor (since 15 Oct 2026, at the user's request; `SplitColor` in `Widgets/PaperPalette.swift`, ten fixed swatches, emerald the default): a trained day takes the colour of that day's split on Home's week line, Profile's calendar and the widgets. It syncs as `gb_splits.color` (`supabase/migrations/20261015000100_split_colors.sql`). Outside those day dots, emerald stays the only signal.
- Keep splits optional, preserve their IDs on edits and compare progress only within the same split and exercise, with rep count or load held constant.
- Demo loading must be explicit, idempotent and never overwrite existing local work. Keep the sample-history marker visible on Home.
- Blocking is real since 9 Oct 2026: Apple Screen Time (FamilyControls + ManagedSettings, `AppBlocking.swift`) shields the apps chosen in the system picker from Start workout to Finish and lifts while paused; the shield screen is the `GymBlockShield` extension, its button the `GymBlockShieldAction` extension. The choice is opaque tokens kept on the iPhone (never synced or sent to analytics). It needs the Family Controls entitlement (distribution approval from Apple before release) and works only on a real iPhone; the simulator stops at the passcode prompt. Never claim it works on a device until checked on one. Billing is real only once the RevenueCat and App Store Connect steps in `supabase/README.md` are done.
