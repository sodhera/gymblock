# GymBlock workspace instructions

- Native Swift/SwiftUI only. `project.yml` is the XcodeGen source of truth; regenerate when adding files or changing configuration.
- Keep the app extremely simple. Since 8 Oct 2026 it has an account: Sign in with Apple or Google through Supabase Auth (project `tlcmpgxuyngsbjuhring`, tables prefixed `gb_`, schema in `supabase/migrations/`, dashboard steps in `supabase/README.md`), every workout, set, split, answer and setting synced to the account (`CloudSync.swift`), first-party analytics of every screen, tap, choice, set, purchase and error into `gb_events` (`Analytics.swift`; never names, free text or emails), and GymBlock Pro through RevenueCat (`Subscriptions.swift`, USD 4.99/month or 49.99/year, entitlement `pro`). Development runs (`--demo`, `--skip-onboarding`, `--ui-reset`, `-journeyStep`, `-workoutStage`) are offline: no account, no analytics, device-only Log out / Delete. With an account, Log out clears the device copy (it returns at the next sign-in) and Delete account calls `gb_delete_account()` then wipes the device. No other SDKs.
